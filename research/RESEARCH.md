# Physical-collectible gacha: what we learned

Sources: Claude's own research plus Codex (gpt-6.1-sol) in `codex-research.md`, which cites every figure. Checked 14 Oct 2026.

## The market in five numbers

| Measure | Figure | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Tokenized-card gacha sales, top 7 platforms | $230M in May 2026, up from $32M a year earlier | Messari via Decrypt |
| Solana share of onchain TCG gacha volume | 64 to 75% | Tokens on Solana, Solana Foundation |
| Collector Crypt lifetime volume | $1.6B; gacha is 90 to 95% of its business | Solana Foundation, Decrypt |
| Courtyard monthly sales | about $50M a month by July 2025 ($30M Series A) | Fortune |
| Graded cards per year | PSA alone graded 19M+ in 2025 (2M in 2020) | Solana Foundation, GemRate |

The model is proven at scale. Nobody does it for arms and armor.

## How the competitors work

| Platform | Price tiers | Buyback | What stands out |
|---|---|---|---|
| Collector Crypt (Solana) | $25 to $5,000 | 85 to 93%, 72 h | Odds bands on each machine, EV shown ($55 on a $50 pack), Turbo auto-sells commons, live chat, points to free packs |
| Courtyard (Polygon) | $15 to $5,000 | 90% FMV, 7 days | Beautiful foil pack art, "Just Pulled" feed, influencer rip nights; odds hidden behind a "Distribution" toggle |
| Phygitals (Solana) | $15 to $250 | 85 to 92% | Public demo spins before signup, value-band odds, pack parties |
| GameStop Power Packs | $25 to $10,000 | 90% minus 6% fee | Retail brand; record $30K buyback; criticized for valuations |
| Beezie, Arena Club, Rip.fun | $25 to $2,500 | up to 90% | Claw machine (Beezie), paid floor guarantee (Arena), real sealed packs opened on camera (Rip.fun) |
| Japanese oripa, Ichiban Kuji | $2 to $5,000 | points, not cash | Finite boxes with visible remaining prizes and a "Last One" prize |

Typical odds shape (Collector Crypt $50 Elite): 80% common ($30 to 60), 15% uncommon ($60 to 110), 4% rare ($110 to 250), 1% epic ($250+). Cash return after buyback is about 88 to 94% of price.

## What players complain about

| Complaint | Our answer |
|---|---|
| Wallet or account wall before trying | Free trial chest before any sign-in |
| Valuations feel low or opaque | Value and exact sell-back offer side by side, with comps and a valuation date |
| Odds hidden or unclear | Odds bar, per-item chance and expected value on every chest |
| Slow, repetitive reveals | Rarity-scaled length, Skip, remembered Fast mode, batch opens |
| Surprise KYC and fees at shipping | Shipping costs shown before you commit |
| Generic, interchangeable UI | A real world: a candle-lit armory, a 3D chest, museum photography |

## Open questions for the real product
- Gambling law: cash buyback plus random prizes needs counsel per market before launch.
- Liquidity: who buys back a $40,000 sallet at 90% within 72 hours. Needs an inventory partner or a capped buyback on grails.
- Shipping: swords and polearms face carrier and customs limits in some countries.
