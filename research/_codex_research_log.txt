## The product
A "gacha machine" website for real, physical collectibles: medieval and historical arms and armor (swords, daggers, shields, helmets, spears and polearms, armor pieces). A user pays a fixed price (for example $50, $100, $1,000 or $5,000) to open a "chest" and receives one random real item. Items are vaulted. The user can keep the item in their vault, sell it back instantly for a percentage of its value, list it on a marketplace, or redeem it and have it shipped to display at home. Same model as the tokenized Pokemon card gacha machines on Solana and other chains, but with steel instead of cards. We are building only the front end mock right now: onboarding, visuals, animations and the chest opening experience. No smart contracts.
