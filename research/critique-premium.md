# Premium pass: critique and direction (2026-10-14)

## Verdict
The world is premium: candlelit 3D chest, rarity light, museum photography, the serif voice. The interface laid over it is template: gold gradient pill buttons with outer glow on every screen, an orange gradient CTA, pills for every label (LIMITED EVENT, DEMO STOCK, SIMULATED, rarity, Free, ×18), identical bordered card grids (armory, showcase, vault), a crypto-dashboard ticker under the header, unicode glyphs as icons. The opening reaches the burst about 2 s after the seal breaks, with no build-up, no silence before the reveal and no player-controlled climax.

## What works (keep)
- Light as rarity; the macro shot on etched steel.
- Cormorant display headlines with the italic gold second line.
- The hairline ledger layout of the landing's process and trust sections.

## Direction: the chrome recedes, the objects carry the luxury
1. **One button language, no glow.** Primary "gilt": solid metallic brass, 6 px radius, 48 px (56 px large), 13 px uppercase label at 0.14em tracking, inner hairline highlight, a sheen sweep on hover, no outer glow. Secondary: transparent with a bone hairline (22%, 45% on hover). Tertiary: text with an animated underline. Icon buttons: 40 px circles with a hairline. Gold appears on at most one action per view.
2. **Labels, not pills.** Status and rarity become small caps text with a 6 px diamond or dot in the meaning color, no box. Multipliers and prices are plain tabular text.
3. **Vitrines, not cards.** Objects stand on a lit floor (radial spotlight, soft reflection), no border, a museum label underneath (serif name, small caps metadata, tabular price). Rows separated by hairlines. Vary rhythm: one hero piece large, the rest in a row.
4. **A quiet header.** Emblem and wordmark, small caps nav with a brass hairline under the active item, sound icon, purse as tabular text with a coin glyph (no pill), profile shield. Transparent over the landing hero, ink with blur and a bottom hairline elsewhere. The ticker becomes one slow, low-contrast ledger line with dot separators and a single "Live · simulated" label.
5. **System.** Radii 2 / 6 / 10 px only. Type: display 96 / 64 / 44 / 30, serif item names 24 to 34, UI 15 body, 13 meta, 11 caps labels. Brass only for the primary action, the active nav hairline and the purse coin; rarity colors only for rarity. Icons: one set, light weight. Smooth inertial scroll on desktop (off for reduced motion), reveal-on-scroll at 600 ms expo-out, 4 px hover lift with a brighter spotlight.
6. **The opening becomes a ritual with a climax** (see the SPEC): candles ignite, the chest arrives heavy, the player breaks the seal, the lid breathes and spills light in beats that climb in rarity, the player lifts the lid, the piece rises as a silhouette and is lit from the top, then named and valued. Real foley. Longer by default, always skippable, Fast and reduced motion unchanged.
