**2. Must-fix, ordered by impact**

1. **Deliver the free chest immediately. `d/m-05–11`.** “Open a free chest” should enter the trial opening. Move banner customization and stories after the result as optional steps. The current top-level “Skip intro” routes to claim, bypassing the chest. Make it “Skip to free chest” and open the trial.

2. **Keep the complete object visible after the receipt arrives. `m-10`, `m-17`.** The Feder loses most of its blade; the hammer loses its lower handle. Measure the actual panel height and fit the object into the remaining space with 24px clearance. Add a persistent “Inspect steel” action that collapses the panel.

3. **Remove simulated proof claims. `d/m-10`, `d/m-17`, `d/m-07`.** Replace “verifiable on Solana (demo)” with “Simulated draw. No on-chain transaction.” The trial uses a fixed item: label it “Guided demo · sample Rare result.” Replace “This piece is yours” with “Added to your demo vault.”

4. **Put chest price before promotional detail. `m-12`.** Show **“Squire’s Coffer · $50”** above the chest image. Cap the image at 140px and put “Inspect chest” immediately after the economics. The first viewport must contain the name, cost and action. Apply this ordering to every tier.

5. **Separate estimated gains from executable returns. `d/m-18`.** Rename “profit” to “Estimated gain.” For the hammer, show **+$15,000 estimated gain** and **+$13,400 sell-back net** separately. Include shipping and shipped pieces in owned-value accounting so requesting shipment does not manufacture a loss.

6. **Bring ownership into the mobile vault’s first viewport. `m-18–19`.** Collapse summary and filters enough to show the first item’s complete image and name. On detail, put name, rarity and era above a 280px viewer. Keep “Sell for $14,400” and “Ship” accessible in a fixed action bar.

7. **Make speed and accessibility modes cover the complete payoff. `d/m-10`, `d/m-17`, multi-open.** Fast still retains card staggers and a 1.4s count-up. Show final values and visible actions within 800ms. Reduced motion must use a 200ms opacity-only transition for Replay and batch results too.

8. **Make batch results actionable individually. `×3/×5`, not pictured.** Use two columns on 390px screens, wrap item names and expose Inspect/select controls. Show each sell-back offer. Replace “Sell the small ones” with “Sell selected · $N.” Sum the actual rounded offers and distinguish remaining offers from credited sales.

9. **Make the macro reveal show material. `d-16`, `m-16`.** The shield’s macro reads as a black rectangular plate. Limit silhouette-only time to 200ms, reveal surface detail under grazing light for 500ms, then pull back over 750ms. Frame an identifiable detail rather than the generic upper third.

10. **Increase decision-copy legibility and control targets. `d/m-13`, `d/m-17`.** Use 14px for offer explanations and 12px minimum for odds. Give Fast, mute, Close and Replay 44px touch targets. Measure contrast against the composed background, targeting 4.5:1 for small text.
