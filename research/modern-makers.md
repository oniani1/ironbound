Recognizable Albion swords in good condition are the strongest resale candidates here. Collector accounts report sales near replacement cost, but these are anecdotes. Signed Japanese art swords and documented custom daggers have collector markets. Training swords depend on wear. Budget replicas compete with new stock; fitted armour has a smaller resale audience. Resale labels are qualitative judgments, with no measured retention rates. [Collector discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/SWORDS/comments/1g3f5t5/).

Prices are standard-grade retail or published base commissions, before shipping and import charges. Non-USD prices use the [ECB's 14 October 2026 rates](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/eurofxref/eurofxref-daily.xml), rounded to dollars. Regenyei excludes VAT. Steel Mastery's base harnesses often use 1 mm cold-rolled steel; thicker or hardened plate costs more. [Armour catalog](https://steel-mastery.com/full-armour).
